Source: [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/oxygen-os-13-3107095/)

> **Warning**
>
> I) **DO NOT downgrade to OxygenOS 13 after unlocking the bootloader.**
> II) **Before locking the bootloader, make sure the device is unrooted.**
> III) **Your internal storage will be wiped.**

## Steps

1. Turn on Developer options.

   > To enable Developer options: Settings → About device → Version → tap the
   > version number 7–8 times. Enter your password/PIN/pattern if prompted.

2. Go to **About device → tap on OxygenOS**.
3. Tap the **four dots** at the top and select **Local install**, then select the downgrade file.
4. Tap **Install**.

> **Warning** — Your phone will reboot and **all your data will be erased**.
